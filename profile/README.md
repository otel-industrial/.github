# 🏭 OpenTelemetry Industrial

**Vendor-neutral observability for industrial, OT, IIoT, and legacy environments.**

OpenTelemetry Industrial is a community-driven initiative focused on extending OpenTelemetry into industrial and operational technology environments.

We serve as an **umbrella for open-source projects, integrations, and collaborative efforts** around industrial OpenTelemetry — connecting contributors, end users, vendors, and integrators working toward better industrial observability.

> ⚠️ OpenTelemetry Industrial is an independent community initiative and is not an official OpenTelemetry project, SIG, working group, or CNCF organization.

## 📈 What We're Doing

- Developing and improving industrial OpenTelemetry integrations
- Cataloging and connecting existing industrial OpenTelemetry projects, whether or not they live in this org
- Exploring an experimental **OpenTelemetry Industrial Collector**
- Exploring common **industrial semantic conventions**
- Testing integrations against real industrial environments and devices
- Developing reusable **reference architectures and blueprints**
- Collaborating with adjacent industrial communities, including [Margo](https://margo.org)
